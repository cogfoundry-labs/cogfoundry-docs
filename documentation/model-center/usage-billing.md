---
title: Credits & usage
description: How model usage is charged and where to see the numbers.
icon: credit-card
---

Model calls are metered and charged against your CogFoundry Credits balance, priced per model on the [model list](https://cogfoundry.ai/en/models/). There is no separate subscription for API access: you top up Credits and spend them as you call models.

The console is where the accounting lives. The Credits page shows your current balance and the transaction history for top-ups and charges, so a charge you cannot explain can be traced to a date and an amount there.

<Card title="Open Credits" icon="credit-card" href="https://console.cogfoundry.ai/credits">
  Check your balance and transaction history.
</Card>

<Note>
  Usage records and per-request cost breakdowns are documented once the reporting surface stabilises. Until then, reconcile spend from the Credits transaction history.
</Note>
